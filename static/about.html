About ViralClip AI

ViralClip AI is an advanced automated video intelligence and rendering suite designed for digital creators, podcasters, educators, and social media agencies.

Our Mission

Creating high-retention short-form video content shouldn't require hours of manual timeline slicing, audio transcription, font keyframing, and split-screen compositing. Our mission is to automate the technical hurdles of video re-formatting so creators can focus on storytelling and audience engagement.

Technology Stack

Our platform is engineered using modern high-performance technologies:

  • FastAPI & Python: High-concurrency asynchronous API handling media streams.
  • FFmpeg 9.0: Hardware-accelerated 1080x1920 (9:16) rendering with Lanczos sinc scaling and visually lossless CRF 18 encoding.
  • AI Speech Transcription: High-precision audio synchronization generating dynamic Alex Hormozi and MrBeast-styled subtitles.
  • Intelligent Scene Detection: Proportional cadence analysis targeting hook intros, midpoint climaxes, and conclusion golden nuggets.
